Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Amp is a music and video player for Windows and Android. It plays the media stored on your device, and, if you choose, shows your YouTube Music library. We (the developers listed below) do not run any servers for Amp: we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information.

Media on your device

With your permission, Amp reads the songs and videos stored on your device so it can list and play them. These files never leave your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Amp does not access your photos. If you take a screenshot of a video, Amp saves that one image to your device's Pictures folder.

Stored on your device

To remember your preferences, Amp keeps the following only on your device: your settings, favourites and playlists, your play queue and where you stopped in a song or video, and small copies of album art to make scrolling smooth.

Clearing Amp's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstalling Amp, deletes all of it.

Lyrics

Amp first looks for lyrics inside the song file and in a matching .lrc file next to it. If none are found and "Find lyrics online" is on, Amp sends the song's title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free public lyrics library, to look them up. Nothing else is sent, and no account is involved. Lyrics found online are kept on your device so they aren't fetched again.

You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Album art and artist names

Many songs have no album art or artist in the file. When "Find album art online" is on, Amp sends the song's title, and its artist when known, to the iTunes Search API (Apple) to look them up, and downloads the matching cover image. Only songs that appear on your screen are looked up. Nothing else is sent, and no account is involved. What is found is kept on your device so it isn't fetched again.

When you open an artist's page with this setting on, Amp also sends the artist's name to Deezer (api.deezer.com) for their photo and to Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) for a short biography. Both are kept on your device.

You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Updates

The Windows app, and the Android app downloaded from Amp's website, check GitHub (github.com), where Amp's releases are published, for a newer version. Only the version check is made; nothing about you or your media is sent. When you choose to update, the new version is downloaded from GitHub.

Notifications

Amp shows a notification while media is playing so you can control playback from the lock screen and notification shade. It is not used for advertising.

YouTube Music (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account to see your YouTube Music playlists and liked songs. Amp asks only for read-only access to your YouTube account. Your name, email address and profile photo are shown in the app, and this information and the access permission stay on your device; they are sent only to Google, to load your library.

Search results and suggestions come from YouTube. Depending on the version, Amp either plays a song's audio straight from YouTube without saving it, or opens it in the YouTube Music app or website.

Amp uses YouTube API Services. By signing in you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Amp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can sign out in Amp at any time, and remove Amp's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

No tracking or ads

Amp contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not build a profile of you.

Children

Amp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
