What it does

Every formatMP3, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, Opus, DSD and more. Videos up to 8K with subtitles.
Hi-Res and bit-perfectPlays at the file's own resolution through exclusive mode, when you ask for it.
EqualizerA full equalizer with presets, plus crossfade and ReplayGain.
Your library, organisedSmart playlists, a tag editor, CUE albums, lyrics and listening stats.

Installing

  1. Click Download for Windows and open the file it saves.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't code-signed yet.
  3. Follow the steps. Amp opens when it's done.

Updates

When a new version is out, Amp tells you a few seconds after it starts. Click Update now and it updates itself and opens again. Your songs, playlists and settings stay.
