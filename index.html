Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

The tools serious listeners reach for, kept out of the way until you want them.

Pro equalizer

A full multi-band equalizer with presets, plus a live spectrum analyzer.

Every format

MP3, AAC, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, Opus, APE, DSD and more, plus CUE sheet albums.

A real video player

Up to 8K, subtitles, audio and subtitle delay, picture adjust and A–B loop.

Smart playlists

Playlists that fill themselves from rules, a smart shuffle, and M3U import and export.

Tag editor

Fix titles, artists, albums and art right in the app, plus a duplicate finder.

Lyrics and stats

Synced lyrics as the song plays, and a look at what you've been listening to.

Sound

Hear the file, not the player.

When you ask for it, Amp hands your music to the sound card exactly as it was recorded.

  • Plays Hi-Res files at their own sample rate and bit depth.
  • Exclusive mode for bit-perfect output, with no Windows mixer in the way.
  • Pick the output device, with crossfade and ReplayGain when you want them.
  • Nothing turns on by itself. Amp asks first.
